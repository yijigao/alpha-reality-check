# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include using
welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive
criticism, focusing on what is best for the community, and showing empathy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting comments,
public or private harassment, publishing others' private information, and other
conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may ban
contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful. Instances may be reported through the security contact described in
`SECURITY.md`.
